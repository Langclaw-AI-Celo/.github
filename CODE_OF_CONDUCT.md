# Code of Conduct

Langclaw AI Celo welcomes contributors with different backgrounds, skills, and
levels of experience. We want technical discussion to stay useful, respectful,
and safe.

## Expected Conduct

Community members should:

- Discuss the work, evidence, and impact instead of attacking a person.
- Give feedback that is specific and actionable.
- Ask questions when context is missing.
- Respect different experience levels and communication styles.
- Credit other people's work and ideas.
- Protect private data, credentials, and unreleased security information.
- Accept a maintainer's decision after a fair technical review.

Disagreement is normal. Keep it factual. Explain your assumptions and provide
reproducible evidence when possible.

## Unacceptable Conduct

The following behavior is not allowed:

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or deliberate humiliation.
- Discrimination based on identity, background, health, or personal traits.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Repeated disruption, spam, or bad-faith argument after a clear warning.
- Impersonation, plagiarism, or misleading claims about project authority.
- Pressure to reveal credentials, private keys, wallet signatures, or private
  vulnerability details.

## Scope

This policy applies in repositories, issues, pull requests, reviews,
discussions, project chat, and public events where someone represents Langclaw
AI Celo.

## Reporting a Problem

Do not start a public confrontation if a report contains sensitive personal or
security information. Contact a repository maintainer through their GitHub
profile and ask for a private reporting channel. You may also use GitHub's
platform reporting tools for content that violates GitHub policy.

Include the location, date, people involved, relevant links, and a factual
description. Share only the information needed to assess the report.

Maintainers will limit access to reports. They may share details when needed to
investigate or protect affected people. They will avoid unnecessary disclosure.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will review context before taking action. A response may include:

1. A private clarification or correction request.
2. A formal warning and clear conditions for continued participation.
3. Temporary limits on comments, reviews, or repository access.
4. Permanent removal from project spaces for serious or repeated violations.

Immediate removal may apply to credible threats, targeted harassment, exposed
private information, or attempts to compromise project users.

Maintainers involved in a report should step aside when another qualified
maintainer can review it. Retaliation against a reporter or witness is itself a
violation.

## Good-Faith Use

This policy protects participation. It does not block firm code review,
security triage, rejection of unsupported claims, or decisions that keep the
project within its Celo-first scope.
